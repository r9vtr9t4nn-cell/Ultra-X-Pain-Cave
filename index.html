22-week build towards Ultra X Scotland · 1 May 2027
22-week race build · provisional

Four runs. Two strength days.

Built around your current 30–40 km/week, 20–25 km comfortable long run and Tue/Wed/Fri/Sun running pattern. Monday and Thursday are gym days; Saturday is recovery/rest. Distances and climbing are progression guides, not targets to force. Drop a week back if fatigue or niggles build.

Weekly rhythm
Monday · Gym A
Lower-body strength + trunk stability. No running.
Tuesday · Easy aerobic
6–10 km · Z1–Z2 (0–150 bpm) · relaxed 6:00–6:xx/km on flat ground.
Wednesday · Quality / hills
8–13 km total · warm-up + controlled hill reps or threshold blocks. Threshold reference 4:35–4:50/km on flat terrain; hills by effort, generally Z3–Z4.
Thursday · Gym B
Single-leg strength, calves, posterior chain and core. Optional easy cycling only if recovered.
Friday · Easy
5–9 km · Z1–Z2 · relaxed; strides only when feeling fresh.
Saturday · Rest
Recovery, mobility and normal life. No make-up miles.
Sunday · Long trail run
Z1–Z2 by feel and HR; hike steep climbs, practise fuelling and build time on feet. Pace is terrain-dependent.
22-week progression · session by session
Each week uses Tue / Wed / Fri / Sun runs, with Monday and Thursday gym. Weekly climbing is an overall target range, not a requirement to force into one run.
